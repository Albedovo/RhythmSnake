HOW TO PLAY

Listen. Echo. Feed.

Every phrase is two bars. In the CALL bar the beat counts 1-2-3-4 and plays the rhythm with each move's own sound; the food shapes bounce in time. In the ECHO bar you play it back. Shapes fill up as their beat arrives; act when they are full. From FLOW up, moves on the left half belong to your left hand, the right half to your right.

●

GRAB

Open your hand wide, then snap a fist on the beat. Your palm cursor must be inside the outlined circle.

◎

REACH

Move your palm into the ring on the beat. Every valid touch is Perfect, including waiting inside.

■

SQUEEZE

Make a fist inside the outlined circle on the beat, keep it closed while the bar drains, then open on the last beat. Grab and release are scored separately.

◆

CLAP

Hands apart, then together on the beat. Anywhere in view.

▲

FLIP

Swipe an open palm through the outlined circle in the arrow’s direction on the beat. No need to start inside.

〰

SWEEP

Glide along the path and pass each numbered dot on its beat. Every dot scores; half the dots keep the sweep alive.

Scoring

Perfect 100 · Good 50 · Miss 0, times your GROOVE: ×2 from 10 combo, ×3 from 25, ×4 from 50. Accuracy counts Perfect as 1 and Good as ½. Rank S ≥ 95% · A ≥ 88% · B ≥ 75% · C ≥ 60%. Every run plays to the end and is settled on its final score. Clear a track with at least 60% accuracy.

Trackpad / touch: move into rings for Reach and Sweep, Z or click for Grab (hold for Squeeze), X for the other hand's Grab while squeezing, Space for Clap, ← → for Flip.
